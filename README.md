An entry for the [Funniest Cat Voicemail Greeting](https://make.fun/competitions/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-cat-voicemail-greeting-8eaad443) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The entry

One line, 114 characters — a voicemail greeting recorded by Miso the cat, who has just knocked your phone off the table and will only call you back at 4 a.m. when he's hungry:

> Hi, it's Miso. I knocked your phone off the table, so meow after the beep - I'll call you back at 4 a.m. for food.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the greeting to `entry.txt` in `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), with no trailing newline.

## About

Built for the make.fun bounty "Funniest Cat Voicemail Greeting" — one line, at most 120 characters, judged by a house judge model in pairwise games. Winner takes $2.
